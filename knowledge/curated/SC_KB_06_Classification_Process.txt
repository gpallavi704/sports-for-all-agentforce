WHO MAKES THE DECISION?
Official classifiers assign parafencing classifications. Sport Compass can explain where to find the process, but cannot classify a person or decide whether they may compete. Event staff and classifiers must confirm applicable requirements.

NATIONAL PROCESS
The source links to the national classification process and supporting-document guidance. The available source snapshot covers the 2025-26 season. Current forms, required classification categories, deadlines and secure submission arrangements must be confirmed with USA Fencing for the specific event. Numerical ages and submission lead times are intentionally omitted from this selective navigation summary because they have not been verified for the current season. Do not upload medical documents in this chat.

INTERNATIONAL PROCESS
International classification is a separate process. Confirm the applicable requirements and submission route with the responsible organization through the official classification page.

SCOPE NOTE (PROJECT EDITORIAL)
This summary excludes individual athletes' names, birth years and classification records, medical forms and diagnostic instructions. Sport Compass does not accept health documents or assign a classification. The user should open the source page and confirm the applicable season, deadlines and secure submission route with USA Fencing. Introductory club participation is not equivalent to sanctioned competition eligibility.
