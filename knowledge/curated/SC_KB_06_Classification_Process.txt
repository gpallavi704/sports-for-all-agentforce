WHO MAKES THE DECISION?
Official classifiers assign parafencing classifications. The page describes permanent eligible impairments and a minimum age of 13 on January 1 of the competition year. Those published criteria are not an individual eligibility decision.

NATIONAL PROCESS
The source describes a minimum National Provisional classification for USA Fencing regional or national events. It links to the national medical diagnostic form and supporting-document guide. Documents are submitted through the athlete's USA Fencing membership profile, with a stated lead time of at least four weeks before the event. Later submission does not guarantee classification before competition.

INTERNATIONAL PROCESS
International evaluation is a separate process. The page directs international classification paperwork to the Parafencing Manager eight weeks before competition.

SCOPE NOTE (PROJECT EDITORIAL)
This summary excludes individual athletes' names, birth years and classification records, medical forms and diagnostic instructions. Sport Compass does not accept health documents or assign a classification. The user should open the source page and confirm the applicable season, deadlines and secure submission route with USA Fencing. Introductory club participation is not equivalent to sanctioned competition eligibility.
