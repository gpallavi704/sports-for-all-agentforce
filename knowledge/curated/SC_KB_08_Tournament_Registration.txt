LOCAL AND DIVISIONAL EVENTS
The article directs registration to AskFRED rather than the National Office. It describes Access membership as the minimum membership requirement and refers event questions to the organizer listed on the tournament page.
https://www.askfred.net

REGIONAL AND NATIONAL EVENTS
The article directs these registrations through USA Fencing's membership system. It lists Competitive membership or an International Competitive License membership, age verification, and the applicable age eligibility or qualifying points pathway among prerequisites. Access membership alone is not sufficient for these events under the article's guidance.
Regional events: https://member.usafencing.org/search/tournaments/regional
National events: https://member.usafencing.org/search/tournaments/national

SCOPE NOTE (PROJECT EDITORIAL)
These are general prerequisites, not an exhaustive eligibility determination. Event-specific qualification, parafencing classification, dates, prices and availability need separate confirmation. Sport Compass explains the process; it does not register athletes or process payments.
