THREE COMMON CHECKS
The article identifies membership level, completion of age verification and event age eligibility as common reasons registration is blocked. It describes membership upgrades through the signed-in membership profile.

POINTS-BASED EXCEPTIONS
The article describes circumstances where a points-based age exception may need organizer assistance because the registration system does not reflect it automatically. This older article does not establish today's integration behavior or an individual's eligibility.

HUMAN HELP
For unresolved regional registration issues, the article lists regionalevents@usafencing.org or the regional organizer. It asks the person contacting that official channel to identify the tournament and event, with their name and membership identifier.
