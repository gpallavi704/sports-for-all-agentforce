RESOURCE PATHWAY
The USA Fencing parafencing hub connects prospective and current participants to introductory information, club discovery, classification guidance, the Athlete Handbook and further-information resources. It describes domestic parafencing competition opportunities, but this summary is not a current event calendar.

AGE AND PARTICIPATION
The hub mentions a minimum tournament age of 13. The separate classification page gives a more specific January 1 age rule and classification requirements. The hub alone does not establish an individual's eligibility.

SCOPE NOTE (PROJECT EDITORIAL)
Trying the sport, joining club training and entering a sanctioned competition are different steps. Current event requirements and classification must be confirmed through official channels. Historical licensing text and team biographies are excluded from this navigation summary.
