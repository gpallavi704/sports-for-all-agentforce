HOW TO FIND A CLUB
USA Fencing directs people to its club finder and recommends selecting the Para specialty filter:
https://member.usafencing.org/clubs
The club finder supports searches such as city or ZIP code. Club owners can add the Para designation to their club profile.

HELP FOR CLUBS
The parafencing club page lists international@usafencing.org for clubs seeking guidance on starting a parafencing program.

SCOPE NOTE (PROJECT EDITORIAL)
The Para designation is a directory attribute, not an independent accessibility certification. It does not prove step-free access, accessible toilets, equipment inventory, available places, age acceptance, language support or a particular accommodation. Those details require confirmation with the club. No real club records or verified local matches are contained in this document. Missing accessibility information means unknown, not that the athlete cannot participate.
