WHAT IS PARAFENCING?
Parafencing is wheelchair fencing. The introduction describes chairs attached to a frame and a fixed distance between opponents. Athletes score by touching the permitted target area with their weapon. Foil, epee and saber are the three weapons discussed.

EQUIPMENT CONTEXT
The page describes an electrically conductive apron used below the waist in epee. It also links to field-of-play information. This introductory summary is not a shopping specification, setup procedure or equipment certification.
