OFFICIAL ROUTE
The article links to USA Fencing's reporting page for suspected FenceSafe, conduct-code or other policy violations:
https://www.usafencing.org/fencesafe-report
It describes reporting concerns experienced, witnessed or brought to the reporter's attention. It says sexual abuse or misconduct reports are forwarded to the U.S. Center for SafeSport.

CONTACT AND ANONYMITY
The article explains that submitting the form allows FenceSafe staff to contact the reporter. Anonymous reporting is possible, although limited follow-up information can make investigation more difficult. The source also prohibits malicious or bad-faith reports; the agent cannot judge whether a report meets that definition.
